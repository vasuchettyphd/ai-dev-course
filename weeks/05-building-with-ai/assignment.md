# Week 5 assignment: Building with AI

## What to do

Follow the [week 5 README](README.md) on your own laptop, in your private `semester-project` repo, then collect four things.

**(a) Your merged pull request.** The address of the merged pull request on `semester-project` that adds your Superpowers plan file (under `docs/superpowers/plans/`) and the first three build steps from your `PLAN.md`, built on a branch (README steps 2d to 7). Its **Files changed** must show the plan file, the code and the tests. The repo stays **private**, with `vasuchettyphd` as a collaborator, so your instructor can open it.

**(b) A screenshot of the tests passing.** Your terminal after you ran the tests yourself (README step 7a), showing the command and the result (for example `OK`, or "all passed").

**(c) A screenshot of your program on your own example.** Your program's answer for your own example input (README steps 4d and 7b), with your hand-worked answer beside it: on paper, in a spreadsheet or in a note, in the same screenshot or a second one next to it. Use your own example, not one from the plan or from the deal screener.

**(d) A short reflection,** about half a page:

1. **One review finding:** something a Superpowers reviewer found during the build, and what changed because of it. Quote or describe the finding, and say what was fixed. (If the reviewers found nothing, say which task had the most to say, and what they checked.)
2. **Your usage:** roughly how much of your usage the build took, from `/usage` before and after (README step 6). Did you hit a limit? If so, what did you do?

Never put a password, a sign-in code or a token in your hand-in.

## What to hand in

- One document with the pull request's address, the two screenshots (or three, if the hand-worked answer is separate) and your reflection.

Submit it the way your course site says.

## Rubric

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Merged pull request: the plan file is in it (1 point), the three build steps work (2 points), and it was built on a branch and merged through the pull request (1 point) | 4 |
| The tests run and pass (screenshot), and the program was checked against your own hand-worked example (screenshot) | 3 |
| Reflection: one real review finding and what changed (2 points), and roughly how much usage the build took (1 point) | 3 |
