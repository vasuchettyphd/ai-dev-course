# Week 4 assignment: Planning a project

## What to do

Follow the [week 4 README](README.md) on your own laptop, then collect three things.

**(a) Your private project repo.** The address of your new project repo, for example `https://github.com/yourusername/semester-project`, and a screenshot of the repo's main page on github.com showing the **Private** label next to its name. It must be **private**, and `vasuchettyphd` must be added as a collaborator (README step 3d). Don't make the repo public.

**(b) Your plan, merged through a pull request.** The address of the merged pull request that adds `PLAN.md` to your repo (README step 6). `PLAN.md` must use the course outline, with these seven headings in order: Goal, Who it's for, Must-haves, Not in this version, Data it uses, First three build steps, Open questions. Make it specific: real numbers, names of things and examples, not "easy to use". Compare it with the worked example, [`example-project/PLAN.md`](../../example-project/PLAN.md). It must not contain sensitive personal data (README step 2).

**(c) Your three hardest `/grill-me` questions.** Copy the three questions you found hardest to answer, and your answer to each. Then write two or three sentences: which answer changed your plan the most, and how?

Never put a password, a sign-in code or a token in your hand-in.

## What to hand in

- One document with the repo address, the screenshot showing **Private**, the pull request address, and your three questions, answers and sentences.

Submit it the way your course site says.

## Rubric

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| The repo is private (screenshot shows the **Private** label) and `vasuchettyphd` is added as a collaborator | 3 |
| `PLAN.md` follows the seven-heading outline, is specific and free of sensitive data, and was merged through a pull request | 4 |
| Three real `/grill-me` questions with your answers, and a clear account of how one of them changed your plan | 3 |
