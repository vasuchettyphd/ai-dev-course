# Week 1 assignment: What AI is

## What to do

Use this week's ideas on your own life. You don't need to build anything.

**(a) Three problems.** List 3 problems from your work, studies or life where AI might help. Tag each one **good fit** or **poor fit** for a next-word predictor, and give one sentence why.

For example: "Drafting a polite reply to a customer complaint: good fit, because there are many reasonable ways to word it and I'll read it before sending." Or: "Telling me today's exchange rate: poor fit, because the model predicts likely words from text it read in the past and doesn't look anything up."

**(b) One surprise.** Write one paragraph on the most surprising thing you saw in the [explorer](explorer/index.html). Quote a number from it (for example a percentage or a token count), and explain in your own words what that number shows.

## What to hand in

- One document, about one page long, with part (a) and part (b).

## Rubric

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Three problems, each tagged good fit or poor fit with a reason | 4 |
| The reasons show you understand next-word prediction | 3 |
| The reflection quotes a real number from the explorer and explains it | 3 |
