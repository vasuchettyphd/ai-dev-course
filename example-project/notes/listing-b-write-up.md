# Listing B: what I decided and why

Fictional notes written for this course example; no real people or addresses.

Listing B was the brick ranch: asking $150,000, renting for $1,700 a month to a tenant who has been there 4 years. Property tax is $2,400 a year, insurance $1,200 a year, and there are no association dues.

I put 25% down at a 7% rate over 30 years, with $6,000 of closing costs. I used a repairs reserve of 8% of the rent, because the house was built in 1978 and the roof was replaced in 2019, so my 10% rule for older houses didn't apply. Vacancy was 5%.

The deal screener showed a monthly cash flow of $430.53, and the deal passed my buy box on both rules: at least $100 a month of cash flow and at least 10% cash-on-cash return.

What worried me: the water heater is only 3 years old, which is good, but the lease runs out next June. If the tenant leaves, I lose a month or more of rent and pay for cleaning and paint. I decided the deal still worked with two empty months in the first year.
