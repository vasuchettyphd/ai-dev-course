# Riverside: association dues

Fictional notes written for this course example; no real people or addresses.

The Riverside condos all pay association dues, and they are not small. The two-bedroom units I looked at pay $240 a month, and the board raised the dues by $15 last spring. The dues cover the roof, the outside walls, snow removal and the pool.

Before making an offer in Riverside, ask for the last two years of board minutes: a big repair the reserves can't cover turns into a one-time charge on every owner.
