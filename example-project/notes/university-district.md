# The university district

Fictional notes written for this course example; no real people or addresses.

Houses within a mile of campus rent fast in August and sit empty in May. Plan on about one month empty each year, not the usual two weeks.

Most tenants here are students sharing a house, so expect a parent to co-sign the lease. Rents for a three-bedroom near campus ran from $1,800 to $2,100 a month this year.
