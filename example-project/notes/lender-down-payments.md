# Lender notes: down payments

Fictional notes written for this course example; no real people or addresses.

The loan officer at the credit union said investment property loans need at least 20% down, and 25% down gets a better interest rate. A loan for a house I live in can need much less, but that doesn't apply to a rental.

She also wants six months of mortgage payments in savings after closing, as reserves, before she will approve the loan.
