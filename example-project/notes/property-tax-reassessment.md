# Property tax after a sale

Fictional notes written for this course example; no real people or addresses.

In this county the assessor looks at a house again when it sells, so the tax the seller pays is not the tax I will pay. A house assessed at $120,000 that sells for $150,000 can get a new assessment near the sale price the next year.

Check the county's tax rate and work out the tax on my own price, instead of trusting the number in the listing.
