# My rule of thumb for the repairs reserve

Fictional notes written for this course example; no real people or addresses.

I set aside 8% of the monthly rent for repairs and maintenance on any house built after 1970. For an older house, or one with an old roof or furnace, I use 10% instead.

The reserve is money I don't spend: it sits in a separate savings account until the water heater fails or a tenant moves out and the walls need paint.
