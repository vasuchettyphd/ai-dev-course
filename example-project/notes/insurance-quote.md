# Insurance quote for a rental

Fictional notes written for this course example; no real people or addresses.

A landlord policy costs more than a homeowner policy on the same house. For listing B, the $150,000 brick ranch, the agent quoted a premium of $1,200 a year, with a $1,000 deductible.

That $1,200 includes loss of rent cover, which pays the rent for a few months if a fire makes the house unlivable. The cover added about $200 to the premium, and I think it is worth it.
